For LC-MS datasets, filters are applied to ensure that only high quality features are retained, for more details see: Lewis et al. 2016 In brief, features are filtered on three key criteria:

  1. Relative Standard Deviation, RSD (calculated across repeat injections of the SR sample), assesses that features are measured precisely
  2. Correlation to dilution factor (calculated from SR samples acquired at known dilution factors), assess that features are measured relative to their true abundance
  3. Comparing RSD in SR and SS, assesses that the biological variance (RSD in SS) exceeds the analytical variance (RSD in SR)
For more details see: Feature Quality Assessment

Any observations can be interpreted qualitatively, for more confidence in feature quality and robust analyses, larger sample numbers/volumes should be collected and analysed if possible.
